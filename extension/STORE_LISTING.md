## Detailed Description
APTV Fact Check analyzes any YouTube video in real-time with AI-powered fact checking, credibility scoring, and channel intelligence.

WHAT IT DOES:
- Extracts every claim from a video's transcript
- Fact-checks claims against verified knowledge bases
- Grades videos A through F on accuracy, BS level, clickbait, originality, and value
- Detects sock puppet accounts in comment sections
- Researches channel owners — controversies, revenue streams, trust indicators
- Finds copied/repackaged content from other sources

HOW IT WORKS:
Click the APTV logo on any YouTube video page. A side panel opens with 6 tabs:
- OVERVIEW: Speedometer gauges, grade, verdict breakdown, red flag alerts
- CLAIMS: Every extracted claim with verified/false/unverified verdicts
- COMMENTS: Comment health score, sock puppet detection, commenter credibility
- INTEL: Channel owner research, controversies, revenue analysis, trust indicators
- SOURCES: Originality score, matching content from the web
- STATS: Video tags, engagement metrics, description analysis

WORKS ON ANY NICHE:
Gambling, finance, health, tech, politics, crypto, fitness — APTV Fact Check uses AI to verify claims in any domain.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUT:
Ctrl+Shift+F (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+F (Mac) to toggle the panel.

PRIVACY:
We only analyze videos you explicitly click "Analyze" on. No data is collected while browsing. See our privacy policy for details.
