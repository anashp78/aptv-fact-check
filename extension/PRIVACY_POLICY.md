# APTV Fact Check — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 2, 2026

## What We Collect

APTV Fact Check only processes data when you explicitly click "Analyze This Video."

**When you analyze a video, we process:**
- The video's transcript (auto-generated captions from YouTube)
- The video's publicly visible comments
- The channel name (for channel intelligence research)
- The video title and URL

**We do NOT collect:**
- Your browsing history
- Data from videos you don't analyze
- Your YouTube account credentials
- Personal information unless you create an APTV account

## How We Use Data

- Video transcripts are sent to our AI analysis API to extract and verify claims
- Comments are analyzed for authenticity and sock puppet patterns
- Channel names are used for web research to compile channel intelligence reports
- Results are cached on our servers to avoid re-analyzing the same video

## Data Storage

- Analysis results are stored on our servers to provide cached results and historical tracking
- We do not sell or share your analysis data with third parties
- You can request deletion of your data by contacting support@advantageplayertv.com

## Third-Party Services

- **DeepSeek AI** — processes transcripts for claim extraction and fact checking
- **Tavily** — web search for channel research and source detection
- **YouTube Data API** — fetches publicly available comments
